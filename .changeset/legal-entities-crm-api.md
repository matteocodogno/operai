The issuer of an offer now comes from the legal entities registry in auth, not from the CRM's own settings: the legal name, address (in the document's language), VAT number and IBAN print on the wizard context, generation, edit and revise, the previews and the classic PDF are read from the registry, with a 30 s / 60 s cache. An entity whose registry details are incomplete answers 409 `legal_entity_incomplete` (with `entity` and `missing`) and uses no number; an unreadable registry answers 503 `legal_entities_unavailable` with `Retry-After: 5`. A missing CRM profile row no longer blocks an offer (place Lugano, no terms, a plain classic page); `entity_profile_missing` stays only on the two classic letterhead upload routes. The wizard context gains `issuerAddress` in Italian and English. Offer snapshots record the `legalEntityVersion` they were made from. The service path sends content version 2 without the issuer's details, pinned to the registry version it was built from, and does the work again once, after waiting past the layout service's refresh window, when the service reports a different version; a second mismatch, or an older service, answers 503 `layout_service_unavailable`. Entity settings now keep only the offer terms and the place of issue: the legal name, address, VAT number and IBAN keys are accepted and ignored. Nothing is dropped yet: the CRM's legal columns, the Italian-only `issuerAddressLines` field and the ignored legacy keys are removed in the next release (release step 6), once the registry has been the only source for a while.
