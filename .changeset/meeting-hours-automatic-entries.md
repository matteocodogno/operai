The hours of a meeting are recorded automatically for the people who took part (specs/20261112-meeting-hours, ADR 20261124-meeting-hours-facts-pushed-by-their-owner-as-a-versioned-state-built-at-send-time-through-an-until-delivered-outbox-and-applied-by-hours-api-only-when-not-older, ADR 20261124-a-system-written-time-entry-follows-its-source-fact-only-until-its-person-changes-it-and-is-never-written-again-after-the-person-removed-it). Once a meeting recorded or uploaded in Meetings has a duration, its creator and each Operai participant get an entry on the meeting, on its day, for its duration rounded to the nearest 30 minutes, with no suggestion and no confirmation. It is the person's own entry from the start (My hours, the totals, the filters, the export and the deal's figures count it) and is marked "From meeting" ("Da riunione") wherever entries are listed one by one. Until its person changes it, it follows its meeting: a new date or duration, the person taken off the meeting, the meeting deleted. Once changed it is left alone, once deleted it is never recorded again, and a person who already had an entry on the meeting gets none. A typed or pasted meeting (no duration), a CRM activity, a meeting that started before the release day and a day that has not come yet record nothing, and nobody is notified.

- **auth:** the service scope `meeting-hours-notices.send`, held by the `meetings-api` client only (`notifications.push host-notices.send meeting-hours-notices.send`). The scope is a deliberate addition to the allowlist, so in production it is added to the `meetings-api` entry of `SERVICE_CLIENTS` only after this version of auth is live; an unknown scope stops auth at startup.
- **hours-api:** the new route `POST /system/meeting-hours/v1/notices`, its first that takes a service credential (scope `meeting-hours-notices.send`, `sub` `system:meetings-api` only, `aud` = `SERVICE_AUDIENCE`, one identical 401 for everything else, strict body). A time entry gains `origin` (`person` or `meeting`), and `TimeEntryView` returns it. A person's edit of an automatic entry stops it following its meeting, and deleting one blocks it from coming back. One migration, which also stamps the release day: a meeting that started before it records nothing.
- **meetings-api:** each change to a meeting's start, duration or Operai participants (and its deletion) is recorded, in the same transaction, in a content-free outbox and delivered to hours-api until it is accepted (30 s, 2 min, 10 min, then every 30 min), the body built when it is sent: no title, no content, no external participant. One migration, which also enqueues the facts of every meeting with a duration that started in the last 14 days. New variable `HOURS_API_URL`, required in production.
- **hours-ui:** the "From meeting" mark in the List, in a cell's entries and in Everyone's hours, and a sentence in the delete confirmation of an automatic entry saying Operai will not record it again. English and Italian.
- **Operators:** set `HOURS_API_URL` on `meetings-api` before the merge, deploy `auth` first and add the scope to the `meetings-api` entry only once it is live, and deploy `hours-api` and `meetings-api` in the same release (infra/README.md § Meeting hours release). Until the scope is added, notices wait and are delivered once it is there: late, never lost. Informing employees about the hours recorded about them is a go-live sign-off owned by management / HR (specs/20261112-meeting-hours O3).
