"@operai/hours-ui": minor
---

My hours becomes a table view, and hours-ui gains a Record hours button other tools can embed (specs/20261116-record-hours-in-place, ADR 20261116-week-view-sums-a-complete-read-in-the-browser, ADR 20261116-record-hours-widget-through-the-host-extension-seam). My hours now opens on a table of one week, Monday to Sunday, with a row for each project, deal, meeting and kind of other work you recorded on, a column for each day, and the totals of every row, every day and the week. A Period switch (Week | Month) shows a calendar month instead, a column for each of its days, scrolling sideways with the row names and the Total kept in view. Previous, This week or This month and Next move between periods, and Next stops at the current one; a date field, up to today, shows the week or the month holding the day you pick; the same category, work type, Recorded on, project and deal filters as the List apply to both periods and stay when you switch. A Table | List switch at the top opens the List, as My hours used to be, and `/hours?view=list` opens it directly. A cell with hours opens its entries with the list's Edit and Delete and an Add hours button; an empty cell does nothing; every figure follows a change without reloading. The figures are added up in the browser from one complete read of the period, so a page that fails shows an error and never a partial table. Every hours page, My hours in either view and period, Everyone's hours and Settings, now uses the full width, so switching view, period or tab does not resize the page. The month's pinned columns need the shell deployed with the new `@operai/ui` first; on an older shell the month shows unpinned. The remote also exposes `./RecordHours`: a button that opens the same entry dialog with the project, the deal or the task fixed and today as the day, records through the same API and rules as My hours, and says what it recorded with a link to My hours. Copy is English and Italian.
