Hardening of the legal-entity registry. A person who has been deleted is refused everywhere in it: reads answer 401, history and version views answer 403, the list of entities they can manage is empty and they cannot save, even if their session is still open. Saving and restoring share a limit per person (10 in 10 minutes by default) that answers 429 `too_many_requests` with `Retry-After`, counted after the grant check; the history is paged (`limit` 1 to 100, default 50, and a `before` cursor, with `nextBefore` in the answer), and any other query parameter answers 422. New route `POST /legal-entities/{entity}/versions/check` shows what a save would change (the changed fields, and whether the legal name, VAT number or IBAN among them) without writing anything, with its own limit (60 in 10 minutes by default) and the same refusals as a save. The import report now shows each entity's IBAN by its last 4 characters (`IBAN …1234`, `IBAN set` for 8 characters or fewer, `IBAN none`) so it can be checked against the bank's letter, and its audit row names who last edited the CRM row (`updatedBy`). The tests that commit real transactions run only against a local database and say so when they skip. The `crm-api` service client is documented with the `letterhead:render` scope.
