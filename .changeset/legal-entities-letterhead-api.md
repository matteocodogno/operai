Rendered documents now print the issuer's legal name, address, VAT number, IBAN and contacts from the legal entities registry in auth, in the document's language, replacing any company details sent with the request. Content version 2 is new: it carries an optional `legalEntityVersion` pin and no company details; version 1 still works, with its company details ignored in favour of the registry. An empty value in the registry prints as a missing placeholder. When the registry cannot be read the render answers 503 `legal_entities_unavailable` with `Retry-After: 5` and makes no job. A pin that differs from the registry's version answers 409 `legal_entity_version_mismatch` with `currentVersion`; a pin older than or equal to the held version is refused at once without reading the registry, a newer pin refreshes it at most once a second and at most 100 times in 10 minutes per process, and beyond that the 409 comes straight away. The answers carry the version used (`legalEntityVersion` in the preview JSON, `Legal-Entity-Version` on the PDF). The configurator preview shows the entity's real details in the preview language, an empty value drawn as its label in a distinct look and as ⟦Label⟧ in the page text; the answer gains `issuerDetails` (`current` or `unavailable`), and an unreadable registry still gives a preview with every detail marked. The sample document's intro is reworded for real company details.
