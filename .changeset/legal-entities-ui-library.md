`Tabs` and `TabNav` take an optional `wrap` prop, so long labels with badges wrap onto more lines instead of scrolling sideways; without it they look as before. `ErrorSummary`, `VersionTable` and `VersionBanner` move into the library from the letterheads app, so other apps can show a list of form errors, a version history and a banner for the version being viewed.
