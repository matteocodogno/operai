A project's hours, set against what was sold, and a project's and a deal's hours on their own pages (specs/20261112-project-hours, ADR 20261126-a-record-hours-summary-is-authorised-by-the-records-owner-as-the-reader-and-its-per-person-part-by-a-role-the-owner-states, ADR 20261126-a-figure-set-against-another-owners-figure-is-composed-by-the-screen-of-the-owner-of-the-comparison-and-a-panel-about-one-tools-data-is-that-tools-widget, and the 2026-11-26 amendments of ADR 20261112-time-bookability-asked-of-owners-meeting-hosts-system-route and ADR 20261116-record-hours-widget-through-the-host-extension-seam).

The project page and the deal page each get one **Hours** card ("Ore"): the total hours recorded on the record, every person's, and a **Show breakdown** disclosure, closed on every load, with the hours per work type and, only for the project's manager, the deal's owner and holders of "see everyone's hours", per person. On a project the card opens with its consumption, what the terms sold set against the hours used: an hour package (yearly: each 12-month period from its start date on its own, unused hours never carried; capacity: from its start date to its expiry, or to today without one) shows the package, the hours used and left, or **Over by** once exceeded and **Expired** for a capacity package past its expiry; a fixed price shows man-days (8 hours, one decimal) against its estimated man-days, when known, and its max effort; a time-and-material project, against the total of its positions' planned man-days. A project with no terms, or an SLA and no hour package, shows only the total, unless an end date has passed or is near (next paragraph). No amount, price or rate appears anywhere. Recording hours with Record hours on the page updates the card without a reload.

The consumption block also says how the project stands (specs/20261112-project-hours, US-5). It shows the **consumed percentage**: the hours used against the package's hours, a fixed price's max effort or a time-and-material project's total planned man-days, rounded down and shown as it is beyond 100 % (119 h 30 of 120 h reads 99 %). A **state** decided on the exact hours, in words and an icon, says on track below 80 %, close to the limit from 80 % to below 100 %, and over from exactly the reference ("Limit reached" at exactly the reference, "Over the limit" above it). The excess is the "Over by" figure ("Less than 0.1 man-day", never 0.0). A **bar** on a scale of 0 to 125 % of the reference draws the same, with marks at 80 % and 100 %, the part beyond 100 % striped, a mark for a fixed price's estimate and, where the span has an end, a mark for today; a pace line states the consumed percentage together with the **time elapsed** (a package period with an end, or a fixed price from its start date to its deadline; rounded down, 100 % only from the day after). A terms' end date and a fixed price's deadline each get a **date line**, passed by so many days or near (today, or 30 days or fewer ahead); a date line reads the terms alone, so it shows while the hours cannot be loaded, and a project with only an SLA whose end date has passed or is near shows these lines alone. A closed or cancelled project shows no state and no date line, and a capacity package past its expiry no state. Everyone who can open Projects sees the same signals; none is per person and none shows an amount. They are drawn on the project page only and create no task and no notification (those are specs/20261112-project-alerts').

The hours recorded on the meetings linked to a project now count for the project, as a linked meeting's hours already count for a deal, whoever recorded them: in the card, in My hours and Everyone's hours filtered by the project, in the totals grouped by project and in a new export column, **Counts for project**. A viewer who cannot read the project a meeting is linked to sees the entry but no name. When Meetings cannot say which meetings are linked, a project filter or a project's figures are shown as unavailable, never too low. English and Italian.

- **hours-api:** the new route `GET /time-entries/summary?projectId=|dealId=` (`windowFrom` and `windowTo` for a project): the hours on one record for whoever the record's owner says may read it (asked of projects-api or crm-api as the caller on every request; a missing and an unreadable record are one 404; the owner or Meetings not answering is 503 `host_unavailable`), as `totalMinutes`, `windowMinutes`, `byWorkType` and `byPerson` (only for the project's manager, the deal's owner or a `time-entry:read-all` holder, else `null`), with no entry, note, meeting id or title, never cached. The `projectId` filter of the list and the totals counts the meetings linked to the project (read live from meetings-api, never stored on the entry; 409 `project_meetings_changed` when the set changes between pages), `groupBy=project` places them under their project (the new `project-unknown` group when Meetings cannot be asked) and a meeting entry carries `on.project`.
- **meetings-api:** `GET /system/meetings/hosts` takes `projectId=`, exactly one of `ids`, `dealId` and `projectId`; same scope, `sub` and `{ id, host }` answer.
- **projects-api:** the resolve answer carries `projectManagerId` (`null` while a project awaits its manager); the terms view's `hourPackage` carries `currentPeriod { from, to, ended }`, derived on read at the Europe/Zurich day, and the terms view states `asOf` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), that same day, on `GET /projects/{id}`, a project write's answer and the terms `POST` and `PATCH` answers: the one day the period and every signal that reads today are worked out for. Nothing is stored, the ETag does not change with it, and a terms body that carries it is refused.
- **crm-api:** the resolve answer for a deal carries `ownerUserId`.
- **hours-ui:** the new federated entry `./HostHours` (the total and the closed breakdown); `./RecordHours` broadcasts the content-free event `operai:time-entry-recorded`, naming only the project or deal it recorded on; the project filter, the by-project totals, a "Counts for project" line on a meeting's entry and the export column.
- **shell:** the host extension `hours.hostHours`, `{ on: 'project' | 'deal', targetId }`.
- **projects-ui, crm-ui:** the Hours card; projects-ui's consumption block re-reads on the event.
- **projects-ui:** the signals above, worked out by the pure `lib/consumption.ts` (no clock: every day it reads is projects-api's `asOf`) and drawn by the block and by `ConsumptionBar`, the bar, which is projects-ui's own and `aria-hidden`; no library change. After a Record hours save the consumed percentage, the state, the excess and the bar follow the new hours; the time elapsed and the date lines do not, being the day of the read.
- **Known limitation:** a project page keeps what was true on the Zurich day it was loaded on until it is reloaded, a save made with Record hours included, which has five cases of one cause: (a) a capacity hour package without an expiry date is compared up to that day, so hours recorded on a later day stay out of its figures (hours used too low, hours left too high or "Over by" too low) while the card's total includes them, the one case where a figure is too low with no date on screen to show it; (b) a yearly package's anniversary passing leaves the block on the period that has ended, and a save dated in the new period does not move it; (c) a package's start date arriving leaves the block saying the package has not started; (d) a capacity package's expiry date passing shows "Expired" only after a reload, its figures right all along; (e) the time elapsed, the mark for today and each date line stay on the loaded day, so a date that is today still says today after midnight, a date 31 days ahead is not yet near and a date that passes at midnight is not yet said to have passed, while the consumed percentage, the state and the excess follow the hours; a reload corrects each of the five (specs/20261112-project-hours A52, D85, D86, risk R8).
- **Operators:** no migration, no variable, no scope, `auth` unchanged. Stacked on the meeting hours release (specs/20261112-meeting-hours), which ships first. Order: `meetings-api`, `projects-api` and `crm-api`, then `hours-ui`, then the `shell`, then `hours-api`, then `projects-ui` and `crm-ui`. `hours-api` before `meetings-api` makes every project filter and project summary a 503 until `meetings-api` is live, a refusal and never a wrong figure (infra/README.md § Project hours release). US-5 adds no step and moves none (`projects-api`'s `asOf` ships with step 1, `projects-ui`'s signals with step 5), but gives step 3 a condition: the `shell` has no source change for it, yet it must be built from the release commit, because the bar uses Tailwind utilities no other source in the suite uses and the suite's one stylesheet is the shell's. Before step 5, confirm that the live shell's CSS holds `bg-linear-90` (the `curl` check in infra/README.md); roll the `shell` back only together with `projects-ui`. A `projects-ui` live before the `shell` draws every line right and the bar without its track, fill, beyond part or estimate diamond until the shell is live and the page reloaded; one live before `projects-api` shows no time elapsed and no date line. Both show less, never a wrong figure.
