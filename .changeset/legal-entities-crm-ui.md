CRM Settings no longer asks for the legal name, address, VAT number and IBAN of an entity; the card opens with a note instead. A person who manages that entity gets a link to its tab on the Legal entities page; everyone else, and anyone while that cannot be checked, reads who looks after the details there. Saving sends only the offer terms and the place of issue. When an offer cannot be made because the issuer's details are incomplete, the Generate dialog (edit and revise), the classic Generate dialog and the offers card now name the missing details, with a link to the entity's tab for a person who can manage it and a plain sentence for everyone else; in the preview panes it is a compact warning. When the registry cannot be read, a notice with Retry says so, also when the wizard's context cannot be loaded. The letterhead overflow notice now sends a person to Legal entities first when they manage the entity, and to Letterheads when they can open it. The wizard shows the issuer's address in the document's language, the try-preview always uses the sample supplier, and the two sentences that sent a person to CRM Settings for the legal name and address now point to Legal entities.
