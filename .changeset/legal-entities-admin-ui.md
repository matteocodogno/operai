New Legal entities page, shown to anyone who can manage at least one entity, with one tab per entity (status, read-only and unsaved-changes marks), Details and History under each, and a draft kept per entity so switching never loses typing; leaving the page asks first. Details: a form with four address lines per language, the IBAN grouped as it is read, a summary of refused values that moves focus to the first one, and clear outcomes for saved, unchanged, refused and failed. When two people edit the same entity a banner offers Reapply, which keeps the values only one of them changed and flags the rest with "Use theirs", or Discard. Changing the legal name, VAT number or IBAN, and restoring an earlier version that does, now asks for confirmation first: the dialog shows the old and new values (IBAN in groups of four), says who will be told, defaults to Cancel and sends nothing until confirmed; a check that is refused or fails says so and saves nothing, and the save and restore limit shows its wait in minutes. History lists the newest 50 versions and Show older versions adds more, with loading and failure states and focus kept; a version opens read-only and can be restored. The Audit page now shows Before and After for legal entity changes. Section label, audit description and strings in English and Italian.
