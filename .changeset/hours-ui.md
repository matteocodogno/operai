Add hours-ui (specs/20261112-time-tracking, ADR 20261112-hours-ui-ungated-remote): the Hours screens, a federated remote the shell mounts at `/hours`, open to every signed-in user whatever tools they can open. My hours lists a person's entries by day with each day's total, filtered by period, project, deal, category or work type, with totals grouped by project, deal, category or month and an Excel export. A Recorded on filter (All, Project, Deal, Meeting, Other work) on My hours and Everyone's hours narrows the list, the totals and the export to the hours recorded on that kind of thing, so the hours the totals show as "Not on a project" can be read one kind at a time. An entry is recorded, edited and deleted from one dialog; whether the person may book on the project, deal or meeting they picked is decided by the server, and the dialog says why in plain words when it refuses. A project, deal or meeting the viewer cannot read shows as "not visible to you" instead of a name. Holders of `time-entry:read-all` also get Everyone's hours at `/hours/all`: the same view, read-only, with a person filter. Holders of `time-list:manage` get a Settings tab at `/hours/settings` with the time lists: the categories and the two work-type lists (project, deal) that people pick from when they record hours, read and changed through hours-api (add, rename, retire and restore, never delete). The tab is out of the tab strip until the permissions resolve and include `time-list:manage`; a typed `/hours/settings` without it shows the permission-denied state and asks nothing, a `503 authz_unavailable` shows the authorization-unavailable banner with Retry, and any other load failure a warning banner with Retry. Copy is English and Italian.
