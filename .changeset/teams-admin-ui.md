Teams, admin side (step 1): a **Teams** section between Departments and Users, for administrators only (a legal-entity manager who is not an administrator is redirected to Legal entities). The list at `/admin/teams` shows every team's name, active members and state, opens on Active teams, switches to Archived or All, searches by part of the name ignoring case, and creates a team through a dialog that names the clashing team when the name is taken, in English and Italian. There is no delete control. The team's own page follows in the next change.
