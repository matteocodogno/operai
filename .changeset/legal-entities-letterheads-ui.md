The configurator preview now says where the company details come from: the real name, address, VAT number, IBAN and contacts of the entity, in the preview language, with the body of the page as sample text, and a link to edit them in Legal entities (or, for a person who cannot, who keeps them). A legend explains the look of a detail that is not filled in yet. When the details cannot be read, a notice says every detail is shown as missing, that the settings are unaffected and that documents are not made meanwhile. All of it in English and Italian.
