# Refund — accounting backlog

Features that would take the remaining manual work off whoever runs expense reimbursements in
accounting. Derived on 2026-11-18 from the accounting team's hand-maintained register
(`Recap_RS_2026.numbers`: a 2026 sheet of 253 expense rows for 19 employees, January–October,
plus the WellD CH chart of accounts in its import format) compared with what the Refund tool
(`refund-api` / `refund-ui`) already does.

## What the register shows

- One row per receipt: employee, period, receipt number (`MC26/014`: initials, year, per-employee
  sequence), description, from, to, reason, date, km or monthly FX rate, rate per km or foreign
  amount, total, account, account name, VAT code, VAT amount, approver.
- Two columns carry two meanings each: km **or** the EUR→CHF monthly average rate, and the
  0.70 CHF/km rate **or** the amount in foreign currency. Total = one × the other.
- The account name is looked up from the chart of accounts; VAT is split out as
  `total − total / 1.081` (or `/ 1.077`).
- Each month has its own background colour: the colour is the payout batch.
- Mix: about half the rows are mileage (5905), then train (5901), client meals (6701),
  parking (5907), motorway/Telepass from the monthly UnipolMove statement (5906), internal
  meals (6720), and a few of stationery, postage, software licences, transport passes and
  lodging. Special cases: expenses advanced for WellD Italia (7122, to be recharged) and the
  settlement of a debt to a shareholder (2620.1).
- Data problems the tool should make impossible: VAT codes typed two ways (`IPM81`/`IMP81`),
  VAT sometimes empty and sometimes 0, 7 rows without a receipt number, duplicated numbers
  (`FR26/002` ×3, `MC26/00` ×2), numbers with the wrong year (`LG25/…` on 2026 expenses), the
  same place spelled two ways, totals sometimes computed and sometimes typed, 19 rows whose
  period is not the month of the expense, and an "Approved by" column that is empty on all
  253 rows because its formula points at a broken reference.

## Backlog

### Priority 1 — removes the heaviest manual work

Specified in **specs/20261118-refund-accounting-export**.

1. **Expense type → ledger account.** Each expense type has a default account per entity
   (km → 5905, train → 5901, client meals → 6701, …), kept in Settings. Accounting can change
   it during review, and the change is audited.
2. **VAT per line.** An **optional** VAT code from Accounting's list (`IPM81`, `IPM77`, `IP00`,
   `BZM81`, `IPM26`, `IPM38`), chosen by hand and never pre-set (the 2026-11-19 sign-off dropped
   every VAT default); the VAT amount split out automatically when a code is set; recoverable VAT
   per batch.
3. **Batch export for accounting.** An Excel workbook (XLSX, in the accountant's journal template)
   with one row per approved expense, ready to import into the accounting system, with the CHF
   equivalent of foreign-currency lines at the AFC/ESTV monthly average rate. Later: sending it straight to Bexio (already integrated by `crm-api`
   and `projects-api`).
4. **Receipt number generated by the system.** Per employee and year, in the `MC26/014`
   format, assigned at approval.

### Priority 2 — automatic checks during review

5. **Warnings in the review queue:** likely duplicates (same employee, date, amount and type);
   late submissions; missing receipt when the expense type requires one (configurable rule);
   km that differ from what the employee usually claims for the same route; per-type limits
   (e.g. meals).
6. **Trip details for mileage lines.** From, to and a return-trip flag; the employee's
   recurring routes remembered with their km pre-filled; places picked from a normalised list.
   The basis for the km checks in item 5 and for a future automatic distance calculation.
7. **Line linked to a client or project.** Pick a project from Projects or a company from the
   CRM instead of writing the client in the reason (Corner, Ferrero, EOC, AI4Dev today), so
   expenses can be reported and recharged per client.

### Priority 3 — automation

8. **Receipt reading.** (Still open, not part of the sign-off round.) AI reads amount, date,
   merchant, VAT and currency from the receipt and proposes a pre-filled line. Meetings already uses AI providers in Google Cloud EU
   (ADR 20261105-meetings-ai-providers-google-cloud-eu).
9. **Recurring statements.** Upload a monthly Telepass/UnipolMove (or similar) statement and
   get the motorway lines generated from it.
10. **Reports.** Year-to-date spend by account, employee, client and entity; approved requests
    left out of every batch (out of scope today; specs/20260719-refund-monthly-processing
    acknowledges the need).
11. **Import of the 2026 register.** Reuse the legacy import pattern written for Inventory
    (`inventory-api/scripts/legacy/`: parse → plan → report → apply).

### Missing expense types

**Flights (5902) and taxi (5903) are done** (`travel_flight`, `travel_taxi`, 2026-11-19 sign-off;
Refund now has 14 types). The register still uses categories the fixed types do not have: lodging
(5909), internal meals (6720), software licences (6405) and the public-transport pass contribution
(5808). Adding them is a small follow-up to item 1.

### Decisions that change an explicit choice in today's specs

- **Currency conversion.** specs/20260716-refund-service excludes any conversion. The register
  converts EUR→CHF at the monthly average. Resolved for the accounting export only in
  specs/20261118-refund-accounting-export: reimbursed amounts stay in their original currency.
- **Manager approval before accounting.** Out of scope today, but the register has an
  "Approved by" column, so the need exists. Still to decide.
- **Recharge to a group company and expenses paid by a shareholder** (accounts 7122 and
  2620.1). The entity is already recorded per line, so 7122 could be derived (a CH employee
  claiming a WellD Italia expense). The shareholder case does not fit the current model.
- **WellD Italia accounts and VAT.** Not covered by the first spec: only the WellD CH chart of
  accounts was available.

## Link with Inventory

12. **From refund to asset.** A line for office material above a threshold, or of a new
    hardware type, offers to register the device in Inventory; the receipt becomes the asset's
    purchase document (`InvoiceFile`). No reimbursed hardware purchase escapes the
    depreciation register.
